# Dune City 1.0.728

This combined release brings online hot joining, a clearer lobby, smoother city play, and interface fixes to Windows, macOS, Linux and the browser.

- **Hot joining:** “Allow hot join” is on by default for new online custom games. Running games appear alongside waiting games, with map, mod and elapsed minutes. Players request entry; the host chooses an eligible slot, replacing an AI or sharing control where the game mode supports it. The game pauses briefly to synchronize everyone before resuming.
- **Version checks:** joining players must use the same version as the host. A clear popup shows both version numbers when they differ.
- **Online lobby:** Play Online sits below Continue, public games and All mods are the defaults, chat appears beside the game list, and waiting-player names/counts are visible. Your name comes from Settings.
- **Performance:** faster path searches without reducing node budgets, less repeated AI placement work, and fewer city budget scans. Development diagnostics are controlled in Settings and default off in browsers.
- **Interface:** darker building information panels with clearer text, live zone previews including development/hospitals/churches, clearer mod and Campaign labels, and improved sidebar actions and cursors.
- **Game fixes:** campaign AI partner selection is preserved; city placement rejects spice and blooms; threatened harvesters seek safer fields and nuclear plant placement avoids enemy fire.
- **Metaserver history:** accepted public chat and public game creation, joins and starting rosters are recorded by the service independently of client diagnostics.

**Online compatibility:** every participant must run 1.0.728 with matching game/mod files. Hot joining is available through Play Online; LAN/direct-IP play is unchanged. Existing saves retain their format. Large games remain subject to the existing 4 MiB network-save limit.

Android is versioned separately and is not changed by this desktop/browser release.

Choose **Spectate** when joining a running game to watch without taking a player slot. Rejected requests to play become spectators. Spectators can see the map, chat and leave without ending the players’ match. All participants need 1.0.728 (network protocol 7); the limit is eight connections including spectators.
