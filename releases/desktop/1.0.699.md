# Dune City 1.0.699

- Nearby troops defend an attacked base before regrouping. Defender assignments remain active while the attacker is present.
- Launchers keep hunting after short evasive moves and regroup around the fighting army rather than returning home.
- Human-allied campaign AI can attack when its army is ready, with a 60-second break between attacks. Enemy campaign opening triggers remain in effect.
- Custom-game AI grows its economy and army together. Allies and opponents use the same map/spice worker limits; the larger allied fleet allowance and aggressive worker-first spending are campaign-only.
- Campaign allies prioritise early harvesting and bulk Starport imports. Brutal allies can field up to 20 harvesters on late campaign maps; Brutal campaign enemies can field up to seven.
- Revised campaign difficulty attack values and military limits, improved Dune City Starport/repair/nuclear build priorities, and radar requiring adequate power.
- Added ten rocket turrets to each starting city on the bundled single-player SimCity map, protecting construction yards and other key infrastructure.
