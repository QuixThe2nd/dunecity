# Dune City 1.0.690

Campaign AI and co-op economy fixes, plus restored action cursor sizing on
desktop and in the browser.

- Campaign attacks begin from the map's reinforcement signal. Easy and Medium
  use independent opening delays, send one small automatic wave at a time,
  and take a two-to-four-minute break after it ends. Hard and Brutal can attack
  immediately after the signal when their army is ready.
- Dune City campaigns use the city construction planner so enemies can build
  residential, commercial and industrial zones.
- Co-op helpers no longer reserve their opening cash for an expansion MCV
  when they have no factory capable of building it.
- QuantBot leaves saboteurs out of army orders, allowing Palace-spawned
  saboteurs to continue hunting instead of being reassigned to guard or retreat.
- Render action cursors at their original size across desktop and web.

Validation: all seven native CTest groups pass. Engine regression scenarios
cover wave spacing, city/co-op opening construction and saboteur order
preservation through a successful attack. Existing saved saboteur orders are
preserved; the fix prevents new AI overrides rather than rewriting saves.
