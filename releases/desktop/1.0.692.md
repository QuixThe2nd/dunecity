# Dune City 1.0.692

Campaign attack pacing and repair improvements:

- Easy enemy waves now repeat every 1–3 game minutes when enough troops are ready. Late-mission waves can spend up to 1,700 credits, while retaining a home reserve.
- Easy and Medium start their next-wave timer when an attack launches. Surviving attackers keep fighting without delaying the next wave.
- Medium AI now prioritizes one missing repair yard and its prerequisites, and sends damaged vehicles for repair.
- Easy AI keeps damaged troops available for combat when it has no repair yard. Explicit retreat orders remain respected.

For an AI partner that attacks and defends for you, choose **QuantBot**. **AI Support** manages economy and construction only.

Validated with all seven native test groups, campaign wave/defence/repair fixtures, and completed native level-4 and level-9 campaigns. AI-versus-AI results do not establish human difficulty balance.
