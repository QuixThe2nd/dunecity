# Dune City 1.0.682

Campaign enemies respond to attacks, reinforce threatened bases and harvesters,
and launch waves suited to their difficulty. Easy and Medium stagger smaller
attacks; Hard commits most of its army and Brutal can launch all-in attacks.

- Give idle defenders Area Guard orders and scout with dispatched armies when
  enemy targets are out of sight.
- Build and replace repair facilities on higher difficulties.
- Let Hard and Brutal campaign helpers develop their full harvester economy,
  buying needed harvesters and a first carryall without waiting for discounts.
- Count paid imports and refinery-supplied workers correctly, preventing extra
  purchases beyond the worker cap without discarding paid deliveries.
- Include the restored Custom Game map browser, with its full list, categories,
  previews and preserved player choices when returning from setup.
- Preserve the current menu navigation, bundled browser mods, campaign scoring
  and exclusive building selection fixes.

Validation: 71 native campaign scenarios finished naturally (46 wins, 25 losses),
with no crashes, timeouts or worker-cap overshoots. Every match included enemy
attacks; all 21 late-game Hard/Brutal helper cases reached 15 harvesters. Native
unit/menu tests, behavioral fixtures, browser build and packaging checks pass.
The browser campaign launches and selection works before and after feedback.

These simulations assess AI behavior rather than human win rates. New WAN/NAT
and multiplayer save/resume checks are not included in this release pass.
