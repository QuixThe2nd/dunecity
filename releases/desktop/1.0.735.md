# Dune City 1.0.735

- Choose SimCity or Dune2 graphics for Dune City campaigns and individual houses in custom and multiplayer games.
- Adds high-detail Dune2 zone and special-building art with matching construction and building portraits. Transparent sprites replace the original art cleanly while keeping the existing 2x2 zone footprint.
- Preserves graphics choices in saved games and synchronizes them through multiplayer lobbies, including loaded games.
- Includes the latest hot-join fixes, download and catch-up progress, and prominent joining-player names from 1.0.734.
- Campaign options stay visible: mod, mission and AI lists open upward when there is not enough room below.
- Longer option lists fit within the menu and show a scrollbar, with mouse-wheel scrolling to reach every entry.
- Keeps complete option rows and the selected entry visible when a list needs to resize.

All multiplayer participants need version 1.0.735. Existing saves, settings and user mods remain in your separate user profile. Missing Dune2 artwork falls back to the original SimCity graphics.
