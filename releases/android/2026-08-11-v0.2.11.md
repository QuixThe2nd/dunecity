# Dune Legacy Android 0.2.11

Android ARM64 release based on DuneCity 1.0.528.

## Included

- Pitch-stable ADL music playback and expanded audio diagnostics.
- Dune2R live-mounted unit states, directional damage packaging, and the first
  Harkonnen Devastator proof-of-concept atlas.
- Existing selectable Vanilla, DuneCity, Dune2R, and frozen bundled Tornie
  payloads.
- Landscape touch controls, right-click emulation, Android Back/Escape mapping,
  soft keyboard support, and Android-local save paths.

## Compatibility

- ARM64 Android 6.0 or newer.
- Existing application data and saves are preserved during upgrade.
- Save format and multiplayer protocol are unchanged from Android 0.2.10.
