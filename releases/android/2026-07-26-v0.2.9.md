# Dune Legacy Android 0.2.9

Android ARM64 release based on DuneCity 1.0.525.

## Included

- Loaded-save DuneCity simulation, R/C/I valve recovery, build timing, and
  gravel-only city placement fixes.
- Dune2R enhanced animated-unit layer, Ordos Tank proof of concept, and
  presentation zoom controls.
- Existing selectable Vanilla, DuneCity, Dune2R, and Tornie payloads.
- Landscape touch controls, right-click emulation, Android Back/Escape mapping,
  soft keyboard support, and Android-local save paths.

## Compatibility

- ARM64 Android 6.0 or newer
- Existing application data and saves are preserved during upgrade.
- Save format and multiplayer protocol are unchanged from Android 0.2.8.
